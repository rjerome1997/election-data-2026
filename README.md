column_headings.py
Matches the column heading to its index for ease of access.

extract_data.py
Pulls voter data and pickles it.

process_data.py
This is where the data is actually analyzed. At the moment, we're aggregating the voters in groups based on their voter score
and plotting the percent that voted in the March 2026 primary against their score for both the voters who were contacted and
the voters who were not. Future plans include a histogram and scatterplot.
